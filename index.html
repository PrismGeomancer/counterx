No. 04

How the counter works

How it works →
  1. 01Hand it over

    The seller puts the tokens in the safe at a fixed SOL price for the whole block. Partial fills, minimum fill, expiry, one named buyer or anyone. Take it back any time before a fill.

  2. 02Ask for it

    A buyer puts SOL in the safe for N tokens at a fixed price. Any holder can fill it.

  3. 03Cross

    One program instruction moves the tokens, the SOL and the fee. Both legs settle or the transaction fails. The floor is never touched.

  4. 04Lock

    An offer can require a buyer lock: cliff plus linear unlock, held by the program. The buyer claims it on the Locks page.
